Budget Notebook

A budget app that doesn't link your bank

Download on the App Store
Need help? Email impro0126@gmail.com. Telling us which device you used and what you were doing makes it faster to find the problem.

What it is

A budget you fill in by hand. It doesn't connect to banks or card companies, and there's no sign-up or login. What you enter stays on your devices and in your own iCloud; the developer cannot see it.

Questions

How do I sign up?

There's no sign-up. There's no account or login screen, so you can start right away. The first time you open the app, choose Start with Samples (twenty example entries), Import a Backup or Start Empty. You can remove the samples in one step with Remove Sample Data in Settings.

Can it fill in transactions from my bank or card?

No. The app doesn't connect to any financial institution. It never asks for or stores bank IDs, passwords, certificates or card numbers, so you enter what you spend yourself.

Which currency does it use?

A new book starts in your device's region currency. You can change it in Settings → Currency. Changing the currency only changes how amounts are shown; nothing is converted.

Do my iPhone and Mac share the same book?

Yes. Devices signed in to the same iCloud account stay in step on their own. Data in iCloud goes into your own private database, which the developer cannot open.

You don't have to use iCloud. Nothing is blocked; the book simply stays on that device.

How are savings counted?

You don't need a separate category. Moving money from a spending account to a savings account counts as saving, and moving it back counts as spending your savings.

Some money leaves on the same day every month

Add it under Settings → Recurring and it's added on that day. Payments whose amount changes every month are better entered each time. Turn on Show in Calendar at the top of that screen to see due dates in a "Budget Note Recurring" calendar on your device.

What is the yearly budget view?

Under Budget, choose Year to see every month's budget and spending added up. Months where you didn't set a budget count as no budget, so use Copy Last Month to carry a budget forward.

Can I bring in my old budget?

If you have an exported JSON file, use Import a Backup on the first screen, or Settings → Backup if you already use the app. You can export everything to one file at any time.

The exported file is a plain, unencrypted text file containing your whole book, so keep it somewhere you trust.

What is Claude Link?

An optional feature for people who use the Claude desktop app on a Mac. Turn on Claude Link in Settings and an inbox appears. Claude on your Mac can read your book and put transactions into the "Budget Notebook" folder in iCloud Drive, and the app shows them line by line so you add only what you choose.

The connector tool and setup guide: github.com/impro0126-pixel/bueong-claude. The switch is off by default, and while it's off the app doesn't look at any files.

Can I switch the app's language?

The app shows Korean when Korean is the first language on your device, and English otherwise. To choose yourself, go to Settings → Display → Language (on the Mac, Settings → View → Language).

Where does what I enter go?

Only to your devices and your own iCloud. The developer runs no server, so there's no path for your data to reach the developer. See the Privacy Policy for details.
